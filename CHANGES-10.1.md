# Coaster Lab 10.1 — engineering, security and feature upgrade

10.1 is the 10.0 game with its weak points closed, its saves made dependable, and a set of new tools for players and
for the owner. Nothing was removed and nothing was rewritten: every change is either a small checked patch or a new
module that wraps an existing function. The full account (audit findings, evidence, test results, what is left) is in
`REPORT-10.1.md`.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `coaster_lab_v10_1.html` | **The game, version 10.1** (generated by `node build.mjs`) |
| `coaster_lab_v10_0.html` | The previous version, left exactly as it was |

## What is new for players

### Track Builder
- **Live numbers** in the top bar (above the timeline on a phone): top speed, height, strongest G, airtime, length,
  ride time, inversions. They are the builder's own lap simulation, not estimates.
- **What a change does**: preview a piece and the numbers show what it *would* change; place it and they show the
  change since the last step, then settle.
- **A status chip**: ✓ Ready to ride, ⚠ things to check, or 🚫 must-fix. Tap it to open ✓ Check.
- **Good next pieces**: the piece library opens with two or three suggestions for where the train is now (a drop at
  the top of a lift, a loop when it is fast, gentler pieces when it is slow), each with a reason.
- **🕘 History**: every step listed, tap one to go back to it. Undo and Redo say what they will undo or redo.
- **▶ Test from here**: start the playtest just before the selected piece instead of at the station.
- **Ten more ready-made layouts**: the tuned park coasters that fit the builder's build area, each passing the full
  check (Little Dipper, Cheddar Chase, Pendulum Pass, Timberwolf, Turbo Circuit, Sky Manta, Talon Strike, Nightshade,
  Aurora Wing, Hydra).
- **Sketch Studio: closest ready-made layouts**: draw a plan and see which finished coasters have a similar shape,
  with "Start from it". "Build my coaster" still builds exactly what you drew. Nothing leaves the device.
- Rows that scroll sideways fade at the edge that has more, and stay where you scrolled them. The welcome card steps
  aside when you start building. The phone dock no longer slides in sideways when the builder opens.

### My Park
- **📊 Reports** (Park Office): yesterday at a glance, the last seven days of money in and out, every ride and stall
  (today, yesterday, since built, when it pays for itself), queues (in line, wait, skipped it, gave up, too dear),
  why visitors went home, how busy the staff are, how long a repair takes. Download as CSV.
- **🧭 Park Advisor** (Overview): up to five suggestions worked out from the park's own numbers (a ride priced too
  high and what visitors will pay, a wait that puts people off, a ride losing money, hungry or tired visitors,
  janitors who cannot keep up, a loan worth repaying, money running low). Tap one to go to the ride or the tab.
- **🔔 Recent events**: breakdowns, repairs, goals and warnings kept with the park day and time.
- **Visitors tab**: mood by visitor type, and what is bothering them right now.
- **Short lines**: a ride's line can be set to hold half as many people.
- **Mechanics** go to the broken ride that matters most first. Optional policy: service rides before they break.
- **Visitors notice more**: rain, litter, no benches, a lovely park at night, the best day ever.
- **Why visitors went home** is recorded when they decide to leave, with a new reason "Rode what they wanted".

### Saves you can rely on
- **A save status** in the Office, and a warning that stays on screen for as long as saving is failing.
- **Three daily backups** kept automatically; go back to one from Office → Park (the park you replace is kept too).
- **A save that cannot be read is never thrown away**: it is kept, the newest backup is loaded, and the game says so.
- **Two tabs**: the tab holding the older park stops saving and says why; a background tab catches up by itself.
  10.0 let the older tab overwrite the newer park.
- **Backup files** with a checksum: download one, restore from one; a damaged or wrong file is refused with a reason.

### For the owner
- **🩺 Diagnostics** (a tab of the Owner Control Center): frames a second and frame times, draw calls and triangles,
  geometry and texture memory, graphics settings, cloud, account and save status, recent errors, storage use,
  performance warnings, a downloadable report (tokens and device keys are never in it), an on-screen frame counter.

## What was fixed

Security (details and evidence in `REPORT-10.1.md`):
- Everything that comes back from the park cloud is rebuilt field by field before it is kept or drawn. Leaderboard
  statistics sent by another player's browser used to be inserted into the page as HTML.
- A Content-Security-Policy: no event-handler attributes, plugins, frames, workers or unknown hosts.
- Park loader: thing, weather and style names must be the catalogue's own (`constructor`, `toString` got through).
- Park codes stop unpacking at 4 MB. Shared copies no longer carry the publisher's cloud code.
- Live presence: at most 40 people, positions inside the park, six known reactions, one a second, reconnects by itself.
- Accounts: two tabs share a sign-in without signing each other out; a sign-in link asks "Is this your account?"
  before anything is synced; sync jobs never overlap.
- The owner and moderator locks can no longer be opened by typing the test flag into the console.
- Database (migration, not yet applied): cleaned names and statistics, shape checks, rate limits, least-privilege
  grants, visits and ratings that cannot be inflated by reloading.

Bugs:
- **A crash in My Park** ("Maximum call stack size exceeded"): a child following a family leader could be sent back
  and forth between two choices until the browser gave up. Fixed, with a safety net behind it.
- An unreadable save was silently replaced by an empty park (see Saves above).
- The published park code was forgotten on reload.
- Importing a design with a built-in property name as a section type stopped with an error instead of a message.
- Two flagship coasters offered as builder blueprints did not fit the build area (they are no longer offered there).
- Reports and backups fit phone screens (320 px wide and up).

`src/v10/`. New in 10.1:

| `build.mjs` | Version 10.1, output `coaster_lab_v10_1.html`; patches: test flag read once at start, owner and moderator locks, own-property check in the design importer, `'` escaped, the Content-Security-Policy, a hook in the Sketch Studio side panel |
| `src/v10/js/20_ty_core.js`, `28_ty_share.js`, `28b_ty_account.js`, `31_owner.js` | Input checking for parks, cloud answers, live presence, accounts |

| `src/v10/js/60_parkpro.js` + `css/60_pro.css` | Reports, Advisor, events, queues, staff, leave reasons, the visitor-loop safety net |

No new external dependency was added. three.js 0.160 from jsdelivr (with its integrity hash) and the Google fonts are
the only things the page loads, as before.

## Saves and going back

- **Saves from 8.5, 8.6, 9.0 and 10.0 open in 10.1 unchanged.** No existing key or field changed meaning.
- 10.1 adds to a park save: `wk` (seven days of books), `pol` (service policy), `ql` on a ride (short line) and
  `st.d0` / `st.y` on each thing (day counters). 10.0 ignores and drops them, so opening a 10.1 park in 10.0 works
  and loses only the week's books and those two settings.
- New storage keys, all under the existing prefix: `coasterlab.v10.park.<slot>.rv` (save counter),
  `coasterlab.v10.bak.<slot>.<0-2>` (backups), `coasterlab.v10.park.<slot>.damaged` (an unreadable save, kept).
- A park from before 10.1 has no day counters: its per-ride "today" starts counting when it is first opened in 10.1.

## Things to know

- **The uploaded preview and this repository differ by one commit.** The preview build is the merged source without
  the small My Park balance change `832c1c4` (visitors who can afford nothing, or leave without a ride, go home
  unhappy). That change was already on the repository's main line, so 10.1 includes it.
- **Three other branches have work that is not in the preview and not in 10.1**: `coaster-lab-daily-streak`,
  `park-build-2` and `trailer`. Only the crash fix was taken from `park-build-2`. Bringing the rest together is the
  first item of the recommended next phase.
- **The database migrations are not applied to the real project** (neither 10.0's nor 10.1's). Publishing,
  leaderboards and cloud saves therefore still say they are not switched on. Run both, in order, when ready.
- The two flagship rides (Thunder Comet, Nova Giant) are about 270 m across; the builder's area is 190 m. They stay
  park rides.
